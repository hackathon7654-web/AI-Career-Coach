Scan Constraints & Ingestion Rules

ProofPath AI ignores artifacts that do not represent original engineering architecture. Skipped files do not indicate missing competence; they ensure evaluation focuses solely on authored logic.

block Binary & Compiled Blobs

Images, archives (.zip, .tar), bytecode, object code, and executable binaries (.exe, .wasm, .so) are dropped automatically.

folder_delete Vendor & Lockfiles

Dependencies in node_modules/, vendor/, alongside auto-generated locks (package-lock.json) are excluded.

data_thresholding Size & Character Encodings

Files strictly above 500 KB or formatted with non-UTF-8 character encodings are flagged and bypassed to prevent AST timeout locks.
